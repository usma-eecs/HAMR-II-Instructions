HAMR II Builder Parts Guide

Use this page while building the High Agility Multirotor II quadcopter. It shows what each part is, what it does, and how many go on one aircraft. Every image can be clicked to enlarge for easier part identification at the bench.

Frame
Lumenier · QAV-R 2C Frame Kit - 7"
 Primary airframe structure. Holds the arms, electronics, and payload together and sets the physical layout of the aircraft. 1 Product page

Motors
EMAX · Pro Series 2808 Motor - 1350KV
 Brushless motors that spin the propellers. Four are required per quadcopter, one at the end of each arm. 4 Product page

Propellers
Gemfan · LR 7035 2-Blade Propeller (Set of 4) - 5mm - Black
 The rotating blades that produce thrust. This set provides the lift and forward propulsion for one quadcopter. 1 Product page
assets/parts/flight-controller.jpg
Flight Controller
ARK · ARK FPV Flight Controller
 Main autopilot board. It reads sensors, runs the control loops, and commands the ESC and other peripherals. 1 Product page
assets/parts/gps-compass.jpg
GPS + Compass
ARK · SAM GPS
 Navigation module that provides position, velocity, heading-related data, and magnetic field reference for the flight stack. 1 Product page

6-wire GPS/compass cable
ARK
 Connects the ARK SAM GPS module to the flight controller and carries both GPS and compass signals. 1 Shipped with GPS module

ESC
ARK · ARK 4IN1 ESC CONS
 Four-in-one electronic speed controller. It converts battery power into controlled three-phase output for the four brushless motors. 1 Product page

M2.5 × 6 mm round-head hex screw
ARK
 Short mounting screw used to fasten the ESC to the frame stack. 2 Shipped with ESC

Connectors, battery
Palm Beach Bots · XT60 Connector (Male) with Pigtail
 Main battery power lead. The XT60 connector carries battery power into the aircraft power system. 1 Product page

Cable Harness, FC to RPi
MiniProto · Custom Made
 Pre-made wiring harness that links the flight controller to the Raspberry Pi companion computer. It simplifies power and data connections and reduces wiring mistakes for first-time builders. 1 Custom part

Computer, Single-Board

 Tiny Linux computer that acts as the companion computer for networking, video, and higher-level onboard tasks. 1 Product page

 Small slide DIP switch used for simple on/off or configuration selection on the electronics stack. 1 Product page

Camera

 8 MP camera module for live video, image capture, and onboard vision tasks. Connects to the Pi through the ribbon cable. 1 Product page
assets/parts/cabling-camera.jpg
Cabling, Camera

 Adapter ribbon cable that connects the Raspberry Pi Zero camera connector to the standard Raspberry Pi camera module. 1 Product page
