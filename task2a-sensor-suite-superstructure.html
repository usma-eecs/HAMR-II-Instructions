Sensor suite superstructure before the camera and GPS hardware are installed.

Parts required

  • Sensor suite superstructure
  • Raspberry Pi Camera Module 2
  • Mini-to-standard camera adapter cable
  • ARK SAM GPS module
  • 6-wire GPS cable
  • Camera/GPS hardware kit

Tools required

  • 1.5 mm Allen key
  • 2.5 mm Allen key
  • 5.5 mm nut driver
  • Fine-tip pliers / needle-nose pliers
This semester’s GPS hardware uses M3 × 12 mm socket-head screws, M3 nylon-insert locknuts, and M3 × 25 mm aluminum standoffs. Do not use the older O-ring / external-tooth locknut configuration.

Steps

  1. Prepare the camera mount. Remove any temporary cable already attached to the camera and peel the protective tape from the lens.
  2. Use the smallest screws in the camera/GPS hardware kit to clear all four camera holes in the superstructure before final installation.
  3. Insert the four M2 × 6 mm socket-head screws from the rear of the camera toward the front. Use fine-tip pliers through the peep holes to hold or guide the screws while you start them.
  4. Seat the camera in the superstructure with the lens protruding through the front opening and the camera port facing outward. Tighten the screws with the 1.5 mm Allen key only until the camera is secure.
  5. Install the camera cable into the camera now. Open the black retainer gently, insert the wide end of the ribbon cable fully, and close the retainer. On both ends of the camera cable, the exposed metal contacts must face away from the black retainer.
  6. Plug the white JST-GH end of the 6-wire cable into the ARK SAM GPS module.
  7. With the superstructure upside down, align the GPS module so its flat side faces you in the reference orientation. Insert four M3 × 12 mm socket-head screws through the superstructure and GPS module.
  8. Install four M3 nylon-insert locknuts. Tighten in a crisscross pattern with the 2.5 mm Allen key on the screw heads and the 5.5 mm nut driver on the nuts.
  9. Install the four M3 × 25 mm female/female aluminum standoffs onto the superstructure.
  10. Secure each standoff from underneath with one M3 × 10 mm socket-head screw. Hand-start all four before tightening.
STOP! Ask for a faculty member to verify the camera orientation, camera cable insertion, GPS module orientation, and the standoff hardware before Cadet A hands the assembly off for final integration.

Common errors

  • Using the wrong Allen key size for the camera screws. The M2 camera screws use a 1.5 mm Allen key.
  • Overtightening the camera screws into the brass inserts.
  • Installing the camera cable backwards. The metal contacts must face away from the black retainers on both ends.
  • Installing the GPS module rotated or reversed relative to the reference orientation.
  • Fully tightening one GPS nut before the others are started, which twists the assembly or misaligns the board.
