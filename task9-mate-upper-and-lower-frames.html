Task 9: Mate upper and lower frames

Join the upper and lower frames with the remaining frame screws.

Parts required

  • Lower frame from Task 7
  • Upper-deck assembly from Task 8
  • Frame kit
    • Six M3 × 8 mm round-head screws
    • Two M3 × 12 mm round-head screws already in place from Task 4 at the third pair from the front

Tools required

  • 2.0 mm Allen key

Step 1. Join the upper and lower frames

  1. Place the upper-deck assembly onto the lower frame.
  2. Align the frame standoffs and the matching holes.
  3. Hand-start the six M3 × 8 mm round-head screws from the frame kit.
  4. There should already be two M3 × 12 mm round-head screws from Task 4 in the third pair of holes from the front. Use the 2.0 mm Allen key to tighten those onto the black frame standoffs as well. Do not count them as additional hardware for this task.
  5. After all eight screws are started, use the 2.0 mm Allen key to tighten all hardware until snug.
Hardware check.
Use six M3 × 8 mm round-head screws here. The two M3 × 12 mm round-head screws at the third pair from the front were already installed during Task 4, not additional screws for this task.
End state.
The upper and lower frames are joined and all eight frame-standoff positions are secure.
Use the upper-deck orientation from Task 8 as you mate the frame halves.

Common errors

  • Mixing up the M3 × 8 mm and M3 × 12 mm round-head screws.
  • Missing the two pre-positioned M3 × 12 mm screws at the third pair from the front.
  • Cross-threading a screw into a frame standoff.
