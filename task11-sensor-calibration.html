Task 11: Sensor calibration

Run accelerometer and compass calibration, then enter the measured total battery voltage.

Parts required

  • Completed aircraft, no propellers
  • 4S LiPo battery

Tools required

  • USB-C cable
  • PC running Mission Planner
  • LiPo battery tester

Steps

  1. Open SETUP → Mandatory Hardware → Accel Calibration.
  2. Place the aircraft on a flat, stable surface with no battery connected and no propellers installed.
  3. Run the six-position accelerometer calibration exactly as Mission Planner requests: level, left side down, right side down, nose down, nose up, and upside down.
  4. Open the Compass page and start onboard magnetometer calibration.
  5. Rotate the aircraft slowly about roll, pitch, and yaw until the progress bar completes.
  6. If Mission Planner reports MAG_CAL_SUCCESS and requests a reboot, reboot the FC.
  7. Connect the 4S LiPo battery.
  8. Measure the battery using the LiPo tester on the JST-XH balance lead and record the total voltage shown as ALL.
  9. Open SETUP → Optional Hardware → Battery Monitor and enter the measured ALL voltage.
  10. Disconnect the LiPo battery and close Mission Planner when finished.
STOP! If calibration repeatedly fails or the reported attitude is obviously wrong, stop and ask for a faculty member before proceeding.

Common errors

  • Calibrating on a non-level or soft surface.
  • Moving the aircraft before Mission Planner confirms each calibration pose.
  • Rotating too quickly during compass calibration.
  • Performing compass calibration near large metal objects or power supplies.
  • Entering a single-cell voltage instead of the total ALL voltage.
  • Leaving the battery connected after calibration.
