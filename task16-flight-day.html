Task 16: Flight Day

Complete the HAMR II preflight check, plan the ATAK mission, review emergency actions, and report to the lane OIC for final release.

Parts required

  • Completed HAMR II vehicle
  • 4S flight battery

Tools required

  • Faculty or lane OIC verification
  • Ground-station computer
  • ATAK device
  • Battery checker
Task 16 is a flight-day preparation gate.
Do not rebuild the vehicle here. Complete the preflight check, plan the ATAK mission, review emergency actions, and get lane OIC release before any prop-on operation or flight.

Step 1. Complete the HAMR II preflight check

Mark each item before reporting to the lane OIC. Any Fix Needed item must be corrected before flight.

Step 2. Plan the ATAK mission

Use ATAK UAS Tool to create and review the mission before reporting for final release. Do not arm the vehicle until directed.

Device setup
  1. Insert the microSD card into the Raspberry Pi Zero and power on the vehicle using the USB-C cable.
  2. Connect the ATAK device to the vehicle using Wi-Fi.
  3. Launch ATAK.
  4. Open the Tools menu and select UAS Tool.
  5. From the Flight menu, select the play button to load the UAS interface.
Mission setup
  1. Swipe left from the Flight menu to open the Task menu.
  2. Select Add New Task.
  3. Set altitude at least 150 ft AGL unless clearance is assured. Do not exceed 400 ft AGL.
  4. Set flight speed no higher than 10 m/s. Recommended speed is 7 m/s.
  5. Add TAKEOFF, WAYPOINT, and LAND mission items.
  6. Place waypoints and landing location well clear of obstructions.
  7. Select Save, then swipe right to return to the Flight menu.
Queueing and troubleshooting
  1. Queue the mission only when directed.
  2. If the mission fails to queue, disable ATAK device Wi-Fi and try again.
  3. If needed, restart ATAK and UAS Tool.
  4. If still unsuccessful, notify the lane OIC before power cycling the vehicle.

Mission review standard.
The mission profile must be reviewed before takeoff and must remain well clear of personnel, buildings, trees, vehicles, and other obstructions.

Step 3. Review emergency procedures

Review these actions after mission planning and before reporting to the lane OIC. When in doubt, aviate, navigate, then communicate.

ConditionPrimary action
Imminent collision with people or propertyForce disarm. Personnel remain clear until all propellers stop.
Unstable flight on takeoffSelect LAND.
Unstable flight en routeIf line of sight exists and the area below is clear, select LAND. Otherwise select RTL and clear the takeoff area.
Obstacle encounterSelect POSHOLD. If line of sight exists, manually fly to the landing area and select LAND. Otherwise select RTL and clear the takeoff area.
Communication loss, GCS failure, or low batteryClear the takeoff area. The vehicle will return and land autonomously.
Emergency priority.
Protect people first. Do not approach the vehicle until all propellers have stopped.

Step 4. Report to the lane OIC

On flight day, report to the lane OIC to receive communications equipment and final inspection. If reassigned to a lane that does not match your current Team ID, reset the SIP switches as directed.

Common errors

  • Overtightening or leaving loose the FC/ESC stack lower lock nuts.
  • Leaving motor wires, camera ribbon, GPS cable, TELEM cable, or battery lead where a propeller can strike it.
  • Flying with loose motors, loose superstructure, or loose propeller nuts.
  • Leaving the XT60 battery lead unsecured or with excess cable hanging aft.
  • Planning an ATAK mission that passes near obstructions or outside the assigned lane controls.
